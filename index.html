Coaching works when there's trust between us. These agreements are how we build and protect that trust. Read them. Ask questions. Push back on anything that doesn't sit right. Once we're aligned, this is our foundation.

The Agreements
Click each to mark it read

Show Up Honest

I need the real version of you. If something's bothering you, say it. If you disagree with something I say, say it. The worst thing you can do in this work is perform.

Confidentiality

What we discuss in our sessions is confidential. I will not share anything from our conversations with your colleagues, your boss, or anyone else without your explicit permission. The only exception is if I believe someone is in danger. This is your space.

Do the Work Between Sessions

What we do in session is maybe 20% of the value. The other 80% is what happens when I'm not in the room. I'll give you things to practice, conversations to have, things to pay attention to. That's where the growth actually happens.

No Stockpiling

If something comes up between sessions that you need help with, reach out right away. I'm here between meetings for the stuff that can't wait.

Progress, Not Perfection

We're not going to fix everything in one session or even one month. We're building muscle. It takes reps. There will be sessions that feel like breakthroughs and sessions that feel like a grind. Both are progress.

I Will Be Direct With You

My job is to tell you the truth. I'll challenge you when I think you're avoiding something. I'll push you when I think you can do more. And I'll tell you when I think you're wrong. That's what you're paying for.

Stay Committed When It's Hard

There will be moments in this work where you want to pull back. That's normal. This work requires consistency to pay off. If something isn't working for you, tell me and we'll adjust. Stay in the conversation.

Sessions Are Recorded

I record our sessions for my notes only. Recordings are not shared with anyone. If at any point you want me to stop recording, just say so.
